2019 The year we took our first activation, around the corner from where we still work.

We started with a small crew and a lot of phone calls.

In 2019 the two of us were taking on whatever came our way in and around JP Nagar — a canopy setup here, a weekend activation there. There was no office. There was a roster of people we knew by name, whose homes we had been to, whose families we had met.

Then 2020 stopped everything. No events, no footfall, no work for anyone who earns by standing on a site. Those months taught us the thing this company is built on: the crew is not a number you fill in on a quote. It is people who show up at 6am because they trust the person who called them.

When events came back, most of them came back with us. That roster is still the core of Sai Kripa, and it is why we can promise a headcount and actually deliver it.

So when a company hands us a project, we are not a vendor picking up a purchase order. Every year of this since 2019 goes into your requirement — the good days and the ones where something went wrong and we learned how to stop it happening twice.
